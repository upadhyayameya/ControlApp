# RTI drafts

The state does not publish how much of each MLA’s local development fund was spent, or on what. These applications ask the bodies that hold the records. Fill in your details and file them on https://rtionline.maharashtra.gov.in or in person. The authority must reply within 30 days; first appeal lies with the First Appellate Authority of the same office within 30 days after that.

When replies arrive, add the figures to `data/mla-profiles.js` (`funds.fundAllottedCr`, `fundUsedCr`, `period`, with the RTI reply as the source) following `.claude/skills/public-works-data/SKILL.md`.

- `01-collector-mumbai-city.md`: 10 MLAs, Mumbai City district
- `02-collector-mumbai-suburban.md`: 26 MLAs, Mumbai Suburban district
- `03-bmc-civic-works-funds.md`: BMC civic-works funds proposed by MLAs/MPs since February 2023
