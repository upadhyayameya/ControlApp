# RTI application: MLA Local Development Programme, Mumbai City

**To:** The Public Information Officer, Office of the Collector, Mumbai City (Planning Branch)
**Address:** Old Custom House, Shahid Bhagat Singh Road, Fort, Mumbai 400001 (confirm on mumbaicity.gov.in)
**Online alternative:** https://rtionline.maharashtra.gov.in (select "Collector, Mumbai City")
**Fee:** ₹10 (court-fee stamp, IPO, cash at the counter, or online). BPL applicants are exempt with a copy of the BPL card.

**Subject:** Information under Section 6(1) of the Right to Information Act, 2005, on works under the MLA Local Development Programme (आमदार स्थानिक विकास कार्यक्रम) in Mumbai City district

Sir/Madam,

Under the Planning Department Government Resolution No. Sthanika-0616/Pr.Kr.96/Ka.1482 dated 12 July 2016, funds under the MLA Local Development Programme are placed with the District Collector, who gives administrative approval to works recommended by each MLA. Paragraph 5.2 of that GR requires the list of works to be published on the Collector’s website. I could not find this list online, so I request the following information for **financial years 2024-25 and 2025-26 (up to the date of reply)**, separately for each MLA listed below:

1. The amount allocated to the MLA under the programme for each year.
2. A list of every work recommended by the MLA, with: name of work, location, estimated cost, date of recommendation.
3. For each work: whether administrative approval was given, the date and amount of approval, and the implementing agency.
4. For each work: amounts released (first instalment and balance), with dates.
5. For each work: expenditure incurred to date, physical status (not started / in progress / completed), and date of completion.
6. Amount that lapsed or remained unspent at the end of 2024-25.
7. Copies of utilisation certificates submitted for 2024-25.
