# RTI application: BMC civic-works funds proposed by MLAs and MPs

**To:** The Public Information Officer, Office of the Municipal Commissioner (or Chief Accountant, Finance), Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation
**Address:** BMC Head Office, Mahapalika Marg, Fort, Mumbai 400001
**Online alternative:** https://rtionline.maharashtra.gov.in (select "Municipal Corporation of Greater Mumbai")
**Fee:** ₹10.

**Subject:** Information under Section 6(1) of the RTI Act, 2005, on BMC funds sanctioned for works proposed by MLAs/MPs, February 2023 to date

Sir/Madam,

From February 2023, while the Corporation had no elected body, the State Government permitted MLAs and MPs to propose civic works to be funded by BMC, approved through the Guardian Ministers. Please provide, for the period February 2023 to the date of reply:

1. The circular/order(s) under which such works were proposed and sanctioned.
2. For each assembly constituency in Mumbai: the MLA or MP who proposed works, and for each work its name, location (administrative ward), sanctioned amount, date of sanction, budget head, and current status.
3. Total amount sanctioned and total amount spent, constituency-wise.
4. Whether the practice continues after the elected Corporation took office in February 2026, and if so under which order.
