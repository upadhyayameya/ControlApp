# Addendum for MLA work/news claims

Same rules as brief-verify.md, plus these editorial rules for items about MLAs:
- "work" items must describe something the MLA personally did (inaugurated, announced, raised, demanded, funded), per the source. If the source only says it happened in their area, verdict "remove".
- Items whose only source is an aggregator summary (e.g. MumbaiVotes.com summaries of print clippings) must be re-sourced to the original outlet's article; if you cannot open the original, verdict "remove".
- Remove items based on an activist's or party's preliminary tally/analysis that implies wrongdoing by named MLAs (e.g. an RTI-based count of MLAs' names in transfer recommendations), unless an official body acted on it.
- Legal items: state exactly what happened (FIR registered / court ordered an inquiry / acquitted / conviction suspended pending appeal / petition pending / dismissed). No implication beyond the source.
- Viral-video or personal-conduct items: keep only if a reputable outlet reports it as news and the summary is neutral and factual; otherwise remove.
- Role claims (minister, Speaker, guardian minister, party post): confirm against an official or reputable source as of 2026; fix or remove.
- Rewrite any summary that uses loaded words. Keep each summary to one plain factual sentence.
