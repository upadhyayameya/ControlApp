Privacy Policy

Effective

This policy explains what information ("we", "us") collects when you use this website, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Orders. When you check out, our payment processor Square collects your name, email, shipping address, phone number (if given) and payment card details. We receive your order details and contact and shipping information so we can fulfil and support your order. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Messages. If you use our contact form, we collect your name, email, optional order number, and your message.
  • Your cart. Your cart is saved in your own browser (local storage) so it's still there when you come back. It isn't sent to us until you check out.
  • Technical data. Like most websites, our servers and service providers receive standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type when pages and images load.

We do not use advertising cookies or sell your personal information.

How we use it

  • To process, ship and support your orders, including sending order and shipping emails.
  • To answer your questions.
  • To prevent fraud and keep the site secure.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers we need to run the store: Square (payments and checkout, see Square's privacy notice), our shipping carriers, our web host, our email provider, and the services that deliver product images and fonts. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as tax and accounting laws require. Contact messages are kept for as long as needed to resolve your request.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information by emailing . Residents of some US states, including California, have additional rights under state privacy laws. We will honour verified requests and won't treat you differently for making one.

Children

This site isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information.

International transfers

We are based in India, and our service providers may process information in the United States, India and other countries.
