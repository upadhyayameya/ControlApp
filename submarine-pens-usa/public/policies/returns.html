Returns & Refunds

Effective

Our promise

If you're not happy with your purchase, you can return it within days of delivery for a refund.

What can be returned

  • Unused items in their original packaging, with all accessories (boxes, converters, cartridges, diaries).
  • Items that arrived damaged, defective or different from what you ordered. These can be returned even if used.

For hygiene and quality reasons, we can't accept returns of fountain pens that have been inked, opened ink bottles, or personalised or engraved items, unless they arrived faulty.

How to start a return

  1. Email with your order number and the item(s) you'd like to return.
  2. We'll reply with return instructions and the return address.
  3. Pack the item securely and send it back. Please use a trackable shipping service.

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your return, we'll refund the item price to your original payment method. Refunds usually appear within , depending on your bank. Original shipping charges are refunded only when the return is due to our error.

Exchanges

Want a different colour or model? Return the original for a refund and place a new order, or email us and we'll help.
