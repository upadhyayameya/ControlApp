Our story

Pens designed for the way people actually write.

Submarine Pens designs, assembles and customises pens in a wide range of designs, styles and functions. Our pens come in three main families: ball pens, fountain pens and roller pens.

Where we come from

The company was founded in Mumbai, India, in December 2021 as Submarine Pens Private Limited, and became a public limited company, Submarine Pens Limited, in July 2025. Our pens are sold through our own website, through major marketplaces, and in our stores at Mumbai, Surat, Chennai and Patna airports.

Two brands

SUBMARINE

Our own brand: everyday ball pens, smooth roller pens and fountain pens, plus gift sets. Many are finished in brass and lacquer with gold, gunmetal or rose-gold detailing.

SWISSBRAND

Premium pens made under a licensing agreement with Swiss Brand Limited, including the Luxe, Opulent, Zenith and Eternia series.
