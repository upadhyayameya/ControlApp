Our story · Since 1995

We don't just make pens. We create instruments of expression.

Since 1995, Submarine has been crafting writing instruments that inspire ideas, build connections and leave a lasting impression. Today we design, assemble and customise ball pens, roller pens and fountain pens, along with gift sets, inks and refills.

Legacy

A legacy of trust since 1995, built through relationships, quality and integrity.

Where we come from

Submarine pens have been made in Mumbai, India, for three decades. The business became Submarine Pens Private Limited in December 2021, and a public limited company, Submarine Pens Limited, in July 2025. We're an ISO 9001 certified manufacturer. Our pens are sold through our own website, major marketplaces, corporate gifting partners, and our stores at Mumbai, Surat, Chennai and Patna airports.

Two brands

SUBMARINE

Our own brand: everyday ball pens, smooth roller pens and fountain pens, plus gift sets. Many are finished in brass and lacquer with gold, gunmetal or rose-gold detailing.

SWISSBRAND

Premium pens made under a licensing agreement with Swiss Brand Limited, including the Luxe, Opulent, Zenith and Eternia series.
